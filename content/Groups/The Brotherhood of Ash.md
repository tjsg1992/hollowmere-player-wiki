---
title: "The Brotherhood of Ash"
---
A religious order devoted to fire. They claim to be watching for the undead and for evil. They have occupied [[The Tithe Hall]] for the last couple of weeks.
- **Appearance:** white ash smeared on their faces, and braziers burning with a pale, cold flame.
- **Ranks:** purifiers are the rank and file. Their leader in town, [[Brannic Holt]], holds the rank of Brazier.
- **Actions:** they burned the town's firewood in their braziers and burned [[Nessa Tern]]'s keepsakes. After the attack they destroyed the undead in the streets, burned [[Mira's House]], took her patients into custody, and took [[Jory Moss]] away.
