---
title: "The Cultists"
---
People in grey robes who sing a low, wordless song. In the [[Hollowmere Cemetery]], two of them walked beside zombies hauling a huge restored arm on a sledge, and the dead paid them no attention. When one stopped singing, a zombie lunged at him. Both fled into the woods to the east, where more singing answered theirs. [[Brother Tamas]] leads their ritual in [[The Half-Sunk Crypt]].
