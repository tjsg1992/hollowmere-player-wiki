---
title: "Magic and the dead"
---
- The world has a moderate amount of magic. Townsfolk have heard of it and may have seen it once or twice, but it isn't part of everyday life. Nobody in [[Hollowmere]] used magic before [[Vex]] did in the chapel. [[Father Wendel]] is a priest, but has no magical power.
- Necromancy is known of and considered evil, but the dead rising is unheard of here.
- [[The Brotherhood of Ash]] is the regional order that warns about necromancy and says it will deal with it.
