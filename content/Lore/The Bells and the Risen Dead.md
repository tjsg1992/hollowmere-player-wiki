---
title: "The bells and the risen dead"
---
During the wedding, the bell of [[The Abandoned Monastery]] rang for the first time anyone can remember. It sounded wrong: like a bell heard from underwater, muffled and slow, far away and right behind you at the same time. It rang three times. Each time, a chill and an unease settled over the guests, and on the third toll the dead of [[Hollowmere Cemetery]] rose: skeletons and zombies, including [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]. After the fight the bells went quiet.
