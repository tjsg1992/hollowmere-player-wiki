---
title: "The Brotherhood of Ash"
---
A regional religious order of zealots, obsessed with fire. They say they're on the lookout for the undead and for evil. They've occupied [[The Tithe Hall]] for the last couple of weeks; nobody in town is sure why they came.
- **Look:** smears of white ash on their faces, and braziers burning with a pale, cold flame.
- **Ranks you've heard:** purifiers are the rank and file; their leader in town, [[Brannic Holt]], is a *Brazier*.
- **What they've done:** burned the town's firewood in their braziers, and burned [[Nessa Tern]]'s keepsakes as "corrupt". After the attack they destroyed the undead in the streets, burned [[Mira's House]], took her patients away, and dragged [[Jory Moss]] off for his scratched arm.
