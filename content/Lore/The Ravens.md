---
title: "The ravens"
---
After the fight, a flock of ravens came down on the fallen dead in the chapel yard, pecking at the bones and the bodies. A giant raven, far bigger than the rest, carried [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]'s body off to the south-east. Since then, ravens have lined the town's rooftops, picking at undead remains but keeping away from the Brotherhood's fires. They're attacking [[The Moss Farm]]. As far as anyone can tell, they have nothing to do with the Brotherhood of Ash.
