**Player:** Rajat · **Human bard**

A travelling entertainer, musician and dancer from distant lands, who hasn't really explained why she's here. She's been in town for a month, playing for the wedding and flirting with everyone. Plays the flute. Staying at [[The Inn]].

**Session 1:** pulled [[Wren]] in through a chapel window, healed [[Bertil Crane]] (who died anyway), killed a skeleton with a broken bit of wood, organised the barricade, and talked [[Brannic Holt|Brazier Holt]] into letting [[Mira Vale]]'s patients out. Mira is sweet on her.
