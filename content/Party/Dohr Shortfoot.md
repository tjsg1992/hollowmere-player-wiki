title: "Dohr Shortfoot"

**Player:** Bryce · **Goliath** (who insists he's a goblin) · **Rogue**

A seven-foot travelling juggler, acrobat and tumbler, hired after he performed at the bachelorette party. Adamant that he is a goblin. Fights with a sling.

**Session 1:** cartwheeled to touch the big gravestone on [[Wren]]'s dare and won a copper; rallied the chapel ("Listen to the goblin!"); killed the zombie that nearly finished [[Vex]]; and, in the aftermath, stole one of the wedding rings from [[Father Wendel]]. Only [[Truman]] saw.
