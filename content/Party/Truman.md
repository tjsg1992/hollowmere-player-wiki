title: "Truman"
---
**Player:** Josh · **Human sorcerer**

Grew up in [[Hollowmere]] and was driven out. He has lived alone outside town for about ten years, and came to the wedding intending to break it up.

**Session 1:** Wedged the chapel door shut with his quarterstaff.

**Session 2:** Rang the old bell by the cemetery's back gate, led the party to [[The Half-Sunk Crypt]] and burned open its sealed door, then was struck down inside.
