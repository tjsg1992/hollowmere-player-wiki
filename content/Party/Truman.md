---
title: "Truman"
---
**Player:** Josh · **Human sorcerer**

Grew up in [[Hollowmere]] and was run out of it. He's lived alone outside town for about ten years, and he hates everyone here. He washed up in the creek and came to the wedding to object and break it up. He has a last name; he just hasn't told anyone.

**Session 1:** [[Father Wendel]] recognised him on sight. He smiled when he heard [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]] had died. In the fight he spotted [[Ned Barrow]] trying to fix the door latch, blasted a zombie, and jammed his quarterstaff into the door. He saw [[Dohr Shortfoot]] steal a ring, and smiled about that too. He dragged [[Vex]] along to the cemetery with a magic hand.
