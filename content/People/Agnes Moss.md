---
title: "Granny Agnes Moss"
---
Jory's grandmother and Pella's mother. She died the week before the wedding, and an empty chair was saved for her; the family joke was that she'd make it to the wedding "if it killed her."

When the dead rose, so did she. [[Ned Barrow]] struck her down outside the chapel with his shovel. After the fight, a giant raven landed on her body and carried it off to the south-east. Her grave in the [[Hollowmere Cemetery]] is empty.
