---
title: "Bertil Crane"
---
A tall, bony old woman with bushy white eyebrows: [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]'s oldest friend. [[Truman]] remembered her from ten years ago.

She stepped outside for air and was caught in the yard when the dead came. She hid behind a gravestone calling for [[Wren]], then ran for the chapel window. [[Asha]] healed her through the glass, but a skeleton cut her down as she tried to climb in. She died there.
