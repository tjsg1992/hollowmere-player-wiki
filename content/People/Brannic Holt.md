---
title: "Brazier Brannic Holt"
---
The leader of the Brotherhood of Ash in town: a tall man with grey hair and a calm, tired face. His title, Brazier, matches the braziers his people carry.

He ordered [[Mira's House]] burned because undead were seen there. [[Asha]] talked him into letting the patients out first, but then his people took them away.
- *"Undead were seen in this house. I'm sorry. Fire is the only certain cure."*
- *"Not all undead can be detected by such magic, and not all who will become undead are undead right now. Fire is the only true solution."*
