---
title: "Mira Vale"
---
The town healer: a gnome with round spectacles and dried herbs braided into her hair. Brisk and practical. She has a crush on [[Asha]].

After the attack she saw smoke over her street and ran: the Brotherhood of Ash was about to burn her house with her patients inside. Asha got them out, but the house burned and the Brotherhood took the patients, including [[Old Tam]]. Mira saved a satchel of herbs and went after them.
