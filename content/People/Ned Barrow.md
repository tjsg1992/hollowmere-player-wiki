---
title: "Ned Barrow"
---
A gangly, red-haired teenager: [[Oren Pike]]'s apprentice gravedigger. [[Vex]] knew him as a child. He brought the warning that the dead were rising and struck down [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]'s corpse. He came with the party through the cemetery to the crypt, terrified, because he cares for Oren.
