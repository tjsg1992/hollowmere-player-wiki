---
title: "Ned Barrow"
---
A gangly teenager with red hair, freckles and grave dirt under his nails: [[Oren Pike]]'s apprentice gravedigger. [[Vex]] knew him as a kid.

He burst into the wedding shouting that the dead were walking, after striking down [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]'s corpse with his shovel. In the fight he tried to fix the chapel's broken latch. He led the party to the cemetery gate, but couldn't make himself go in. *"Please, please go bring him back."*
