---
title: "Nessa Tern"
---
A halfling, once a wagoner, who spent years on the road before settling in [[Hollowmere]]. She loves trading road stories with travellers. *"My back gave out before the wanderlust did."*

She can't stand the Brotherhood of Ash: they burn the town's firewood in their braziers, and they took the keepsakes from her travels, called them corrupt and burned them. After the attack she was furious with them.
