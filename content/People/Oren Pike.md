---
title: "Oren Pike"
---
The cemetery keeper and [[Tamsin Pike]]'s father. [[Ned Barrow]] is his apprentice. He went into the [[Hollowmere Cemetery]] after the attack and did not return. His belongings were found beside the dug-up large graves. The party found him bound on an altar in [[The Half-Sunk Crypt]], with [[Brother Tamas]] singing over him.
