---
title: "Pella Moss"
---
Jory's mother, and daughter of [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]. She screamed when she saw her mother's corpse in the chapel yard. After the fight she went home to check the farm and came back bloodied: ravens are in the orchard eating her goats alive, [[Dorrin]] is trapped in the pen, and her mother's body is out there somewhere. *"I won't leave her to them."*
