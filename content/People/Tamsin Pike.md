---
title: "Tamsin Pike"
---
The bride. Daughter of [[Oren Pike]], the cemetery keeper, and newly (almost) married to [[Jory Moss]]. Dry and unbothered by death. *"My dad's buried half this town."*

She hired [[Vex]] for the day, so she's technically his boss. When the dead rose she backed [[Dohr Shortfoot]] ("Listen to the goblin!"). When the Brotherhood took Jory, she went after them. The rings were never exchanged.
