---
title: "Hollowmere Cemetery"
---
Kept by [[Oren Pike]], with [[Ned Barrow]] as his apprentice. Many of the recent graves have been clawed open from inside; [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]]'s is empty. At the back stand giant-sized gravestones, and two of their graves have been dug out neatly by people, the earth piled beside them. Oren's things lie scattered there. Fresh drag marks lead toward the woods, and low, wordless singing comes from beyond the trees.
