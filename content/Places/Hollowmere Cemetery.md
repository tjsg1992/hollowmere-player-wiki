---
title: "Hollowmere Cemetery"
---
Kept by [[Oren Pike]]. Many recent graves were opened from inside when the dead rose. At the back, two unusually large graves have been dug out, and drag marks lead from them into the woods.

A sledge with a huge restored arm on it was left at the cemetery's back gate. Beside the gate stands an old bell on a stone post. While it rings, the chill felt since the wedding lifts. The dead hauling the sledge shied away from it.
