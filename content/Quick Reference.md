## Current situation
- The party is in the [[Hollowmere Cemetery]], beside two dug-up large graves. Drag marks lead into the woods, where singing can be heard.
- [[Oren Pike]] is missing, and [[Ned Barrow]] is waiting at the cemetery gate.
- [[Jory Moss]] was taken by the Brotherhood of Ash.
- Ravens are attacking [[The Moss Farm]].

## People
- **[[Oren Pike]]:** cemetery keeper, missing.
- **[[Ned Barrow]]:** Oren's apprentice, at the cemetery gate.
- **[[Tamsin Pike]]:** the bride, searching for Jory.
- **[[Jory Moss]]:** the groom, held by the Brotherhood.
- **[[Pella Moss]]:** Jory's mother, whose farm is under attack.
- **[[Brannic Holt|Brazier Brannic Holt]]:** leader of the Brotherhood in town.
- **[[Mira Vale]]:** the healer, whose patients were taken.

## Places
- **[[Hollowmere Cemetery]]:** where the dead rose.
- **[[The Abandoned Monastery]]:** to the south-west; its bell rang before the dead rose.
- **[[The Tithe Hall]]:** where the Brotherhood is staying.
- **[[The Tall Building to the South-East]]:** where a giant raven took Granny Agnes's body.
