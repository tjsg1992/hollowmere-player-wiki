*After Session 1. The people and places most likely to matter next.*

## Right now
- **You are** in the [[Hollowmere Cemetery]], by two huge graves that someone dug open. [[Oren Pike]]'s things lie scattered nearby, fresh drag marks lead into the woods, and low, wordless singing comes from beyond the trees.
- **Three people need help:**
  - [[Ned Barrow|Ned]] asked you to find Oren. You chose this one.
  - [[Tamsin Pike|Tamsin]]'s new husband [[Jory Moss|Jory]] was dragged off by the Brotherhood of Ash.
  - [[Pella Moss|Pella]]'s orchard is being eaten by ravens, Dorrin is trapped in a pen, and her mother's body is out there somewhere.

## People
- **[[Oren Pike]]**: the cemetery keeper, Tamsin's father. Missing in the cemetery.
- **[[Ned Barrow]]**: Oren's nervous teenage apprentice. Waiting at the cemetery gate.
- **[[Tamsin Pike]]**: the bride. Gone after the Brotherhood to get Jory back.
- **[[Jory Moss]]**: the groom. Taken by the Brotherhood because a skeleton scratched his arm.
- **[[Pella Moss]]**: Jory's mother. Her farm is under attack.
- **[[Brannic Holt|Brazier Brannic Holt]]**: leader of the Brotherhood in town. Calm, certain, and burning things.
- **[[Mira Vale]]**: the town healer. Her house was burned and her patients were taken.

## Places
- **[[Hollowmere Cemetery]]**: where the dead came from. You are here.
- **[[The Abandoned Monastery]]**: on a hill to the south-west. Its bells rang, and the dead rose.
- **[[The Tithe Hall]]**: where the Brotherhood is staying.
- **[[The Tall Building to the South-East]]**: where the giant raven took Granny Agnes's body.
