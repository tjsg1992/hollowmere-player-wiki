---
title: "Session 1 Recap"
---

- The party attended the wedding of [[Tamsin Pike]] and [[Jory Moss]].
- The bell of [[The Abandoned Monastery]] tolled three times. On the third toll, the dead of the [[Hollowmere Cemetery]] rose.
- The party held [[Hollowmere Chapel]] against skeletons and zombies. [[Wren]] was saved; [[Bertil Crane]] was killed.
- A flock of ravens fed on the fallen dead, and a giant raven carried off the body of [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]].
- The Brotherhood of Ash burned [[Mira's House]] and took her patients into custody, after [[Asha]] persuaded them to let the patients out first.
- The Brotherhood took Jory for a scratch on his arm.
- [[Dohr Shortfoot]] stole one of the wedding rings.
- The party chose to search for [[Oren Pike]], and found dug-up giant graves at the back of the cemetery.
