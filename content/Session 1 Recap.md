---
title: "Session 1 Recap — The Third Bell"
---

The four of you were at the wedding of [[Tamsin Pike]] and [[Jory Moss]] in [[Hollowmere]]: [[Asha]] as the band, [[Dohr Shortfoot]] as the entertainment from the bachelorette party, [[Vex]] carrying trays for the catering, and [[Truman]], back after ten years, hoping to ruin it.

While the guests waited, a bell rang out from [[The Abandoned Monastery]] on its hill to the south-west. Nobody had ever heard that bell rung, and it sounded wrong: muffled, slow, far away and right behind you at once. It rang again during Jory's vows. On the third toll, as Tamsin said "until death", [[Ned Barrow]] burst into the chapel shouting that the dead were walking. Behind him lay the corpse of [[Agnes Moss|Granny Agnes]], Jory's grandmother, buried last week. Ned had struck her down with his shovel.

The dead came up the cemetery lane. Jory ran out to save a frozen guest and took a skeleton's claws across his arm. Asha had a stool hurled through a back window and pulled little [[Wren]] in through it. Dohr rallied the town from a pew ("Listen to the goblin!"). Vex shot fire from his fingertip in front of half the town, then nearly died to a zombie until Dohr's sling stone took its head off. Truman jammed his quarterstaff into the broken door. [[Bertil Crane]], Agnes's oldest friend, was caught outside and cut down at the window despite Asha's healing. In the end the door held, and the skeletons gave up and wandered away.

Then the ravens came: a flock of them, picking at the fallen dead. A giant raven lifted Granny Agnes's body and flew off south-east, toward a tall building in the distance.

Smoke rose over town. [[Mira Vale]] ran for her street, and you followed. The Brotherhood of Ash, zealots in white ash with braziers of cold, pale fire, were about to burn her house with her patients inside. Asha talked [[Brannic Holt|Brazier Holt]] into letting them out, but the house burned anyway, and the Brotherhood marched the sick away. In the confusion back at the chapel, Dohr quietly pocketed one of the wedding rings; Truman saw, and smiled.

Meanwhile the Brotherhood had dragged Jory off for his scratched arm. Three people came to you at once: Ned, because [[Oren Pike]] had gone into the cemetery and not come back; Tamsin, because they took her husband; and [[Pella Moss|Pella]], because ravens are eating her farm alive. You can only be in one place tonight. You chose the cemetery.

Ned led you to the gate and would go no further. Inside, recent graves had been clawed open from below, Granny Agnes's among them. At the back, two giant-sized graves had been neatly dug out by people. Oren's things lay scattered on the ground, fresh drag marks led toward the woods, and from beyond the trees came low, wordless singing.
